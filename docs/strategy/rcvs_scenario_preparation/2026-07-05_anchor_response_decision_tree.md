# ANCHOR Response Decision Tree

> **Documentation-only (5 July 2026).** Branches activate only **when the final RCVS / VATA framework or post-consultation output becomes available** and the comparison protocol has classified it. Every branch ends at a founder decision — this tree recommends, it does not decide. No wording below is public copy until approved; "framework-compliant" is never available in any branch.

Classification comes from protocol Step 4 (orientation weighting) plus Steps 5–9 (theme tests). If two branches apply, take the more conservative wording of the two.

---

## Branch 1 — Provider-heavy

- **Positioning headline:** "ANCHOR helps practices govern their side of AI use — and structure the questions providers should answer."
- **Product emphasis:** receipts + human review as tool-agnostic governance; self-assessment vendor-inventory item; Phase 2B question-structuring rationale (future).
- **Roadmap implication:** prepare (not start) a Phase 2B founder decision memo; core roadmap unchanged.
- **Legal caution:** check whether any provider-facing expectation could be read onto ANCHOR itself; solicitor question before public commentary on provider duties.
- **Wording to use:** "structurer, not arbiter"; "helps practices record buyer-side diligence and evidence gaps"; "metadata-only evidence".
- **Wording to avoid:** "ANCHOR assesses/validates/rates providers"; "vendors must use ANCHOR"; any reading of provider obligations as ANCHOR obligations fulfilled.

## Branch 2 — Clinic/practice-heavy

- **Positioning headline:** "The practice-side duties now have a practice-side infrastructure: policy, literacy, review, transparency, evidence."
- **Product emphasis:** the full Phase 2A wedge + M6 evidence loop, mapped expectation-by-expectation (protocol Step 13 table).
- **Roadmap implication:** the security/legal gates become the commercial critical path; consider M5.7 onboarding merge review (experiment branch) — founder decision.
- **Legal caution:** never imply using ANCHOR discharges a practice duty; solicitor review of any copy that references specific framework expectations.
- **Wording to use:** "helps clinics evidence responsible AI governance practices"; "aligned with emerging professional expectations"; "human review required".
- **Wording to avoid:** "RCVS compliant"; "meets the framework's requirements"; "everything you need to comply".

## Branch 3 — Evidence-heavy

- **Positioning headline:** "Governance you can show: metadata-only receipts for AI-assisted work."
- **Product emphasis:** receipts, review states, traceability, CPD exports with hashes, Trust Pack; the metadata-only posture as the privacy-preserving way to keep evidence.
- **Roadmap implication:** evidence-export/bundle polish rises in priority; receipt-schema work (docs/strategy) gets compared against stated record expectations.
- **Legal caution:** retention promises must match the R2 retention note; no "audit-proof" or statutory record claims.
- **Wording to use:** "metadata-only evidence of AI-use governance, human review, and traceability"; "receipts confirm governance metadata, policy version, review posture".
- **Wording to avoid:** "audit-proof"; "satisfies record-keeping requirements"; "receipts prove clinical correctness or safety".

## Branch 4 — Principle-only

- **Positioning headline:** "Principles need practice: ANCHOR is one way a clinic turns responsible-AI principles into visible, reviewable habits."
- **Product emphasis:** the coherent whole rather than any single surface; conviction-based narrative per Addendum v1.3.
- **Roadmap implication:** none — explicitly resist inventing requirements.
- **Legal caution:** low; the risk is marketing overreach, not legal exposure.
- **Wording to use:** "aligned with the direction of emerging professional expectations"; "supports responsible AI governance".
- **Wording to avoid:** "the framework requires…"; "mandated"; anything converting principles into obligations.

## Branch 6 — Client transparency becomes central

- **Positioning headline:** "Tell clients clearly, consistently, and keep evidence that you did."
- **Product emphasis:** 2A-4 transparency layer (templates, publish/preview, versioned statements); client-safe receipt framing.

- **Wording to use:** "helps clinics communicate bounded AI-use practices clearly to clients"; "dated, versioned public statements".
- **Wording to avoid:** "obtains/ensures consent"; "GDPR compliant"; "satisfies transparency obligations".
