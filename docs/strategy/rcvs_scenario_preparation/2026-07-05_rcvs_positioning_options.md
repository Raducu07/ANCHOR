# ANCHOR Positioning Options (Post-RCVS/VATA)

> **Documentation-only options analysis (5 July 2026).** A positioning is selected (or the current one confirmed) at protocol Step 17.7, by founder decision, **when the final RCVS / VATA framework or post-consultation output becomes available**. Every option keeps the doctrine invariants: aligned-not-compliant; metadata-only; human review required; not clinical AI; structurer, not arbiter. No option ever licenses compliance/approval/certification/endorsement claims.

---

## Option 1 — Clinic-facing AI governance SaaS (current default)

- **When appropriate:** practice-heavy or evidence-heavy outcomes (scenarios 2/3); also the safe default under scenarios 4/9.
- **Benefits:** matches what is actually built; single buyer type; the whole Phase 2A + M6 story lands as one product; no repositioning cost.
- **Risks:** if the final output centres providers or external tools, a pure clinic-workflow story understates ANCHOR's most differentiated angle; SaaS framing invites feature-comparison against PMS/EHR incumbents.
- **Strongest wording:** "Governance, trust, learning, intelligence, and readiness infrastructure for responsible AI use in veterinary clinics." / "Helps clinics evidence responsible AI governance practices."
- **Avoid:** "compliance platform"; "keeps you compliant"; "all-in-one AI solution".

## Option 2 — Governance evidence schema / reference implementation

- **When appropriate:** evidence-heavy outcome (scenario 3), especially if the document sketches record expectations without naming formats; also attractive if larger platforms start building their own governance features.
- **Benefits:** positions ANCHOR's receipt/metadata schema as the durable asset; survives commoditisation of individual features; appeals to integrators and corporate groups.
- **Risks:** abstract for independent practice buyers; "reference implementation" can read as pre-commercial; invites standards-body dynamics ANCHOR cannot control.
- **Strongest wording:** "A metadata-only evidence schema for governed AI use — receipts, review states, and traceability a practice can show." / "Architected for vendor-neutrality."
- **Avoid:** "the standard"; "industry standard"; any implication RCVS/VATA adopted or blessed the schema.

## Option 3 — Procurement and insurer readiness layer

- **When appropriate:** if insurers/procurement reviewers appear in the final document as intended consumers of practice evidence, or market signals show insurer interest in AI-governance posture.
- **Benefits:** creates a paying stakeholder beyond the practice; Trust Pack and self-assessment map naturally; evidence aggregation is already metadata-only and exportable.
- **Risks:** highest wording risk of all options — one step from "helps with premiums/liability", which is never claimable; insurer conversations need legal pack maturity ANCHOR does not yet have signed off.
- **Strongest wording:** "Helps practices show their AI-governance posture to the people who ask — with metadata-only evidence, not clinical records."
- **Avoid:** "insurer-approved"; "reduces liability/premiums"; "procurement-compliant"; any outcome promise.

## Option 4 — AI-use policy / staff-readiness infrastructure

- **When appropriate:** AI-literacy-heavy or accountability-heavy outcomes (scenarios 5/6); strong fit for corporate groups rolling out policy across sites.
- **Benefits:** cheapest story to tell; policy + attestation + CPD-recordable literacy is concrete and shipped; the M4.6 experiment work (gated) extends it naturally.
- **Risks:** narrows perceived scope (leaves receipts/transparency value on the table); pulls constantly toward competence/certification claims that must be refused.
- **Strongest wording:** "Policy, attestation, and CPD-recordable AI literacy activity — with metadata-only records of who read, acknowledged, and learned what, when." / "Not certified CPD; not proof of competence."
- **Avoid:** "training compliance"; "certifies staff"; "RCVS-accredited"; "competence assurance".

## Option 5 — External AI tool declared-use governance layer

- **When appropriate:** only under scenario 8 (tool-inventory/declared-use-heavy outcome) *and* after a founder Phase 2B decision; groundwork exists (question-set mapping; source-note discipline) but the layer is unbuilt.
- **Benefits:** most differentiated white space; directly serves the transparency-asymmetry problem evidenced by the peer-reviewed audit; structurer role is defensible and doctrine-clean.
- **Risks:** positioning ahead of product (nothing shipped); constant arbiter-drift pressure (buyers want ratings; ANCHOR must refuse); depends on the final document actually creating the expectation.
- **Strongest wording:** "ANCHOR helps practices structure the questions, record provider answers and gaps, and keep a governance trail around the AI tools they use — a structurer, not an arbiter."
- **Avoid:** "tool ratings"; "approved tools list"; "vets your vendors"; "framework-compliant assessments"; "ANCHOR validates AI tools".

## Option 6 — Hybrid positioning

- **When appropriate:** when the final output spreads weight across practice duties + evidence + tools (plausible for a 23-principle document); or during transition while a founder decision on emphasis is pending.
- **Benefits:** nothing is thrown away; each audience hears the nearest facet; matches the reality that ANCHOR already spans policy/literacy/evidence/transparency.
- **Risks:** diluted message ("what is it, in one line?"); hardest to keep wording-disciplined across facets; can mask the need to actually choose.
- **Strongest wording:** lead with the Option 1 line, then facet by audience: evidence line for insurers, policy/literacy line for groups, structurer line (future-tense, honestly gated) for tool governance.
- **Avoid:** stacking facets into one breathless compound claim; presenting the unbuilt tool-governance layer in the present tense.

---

## Standing wording controls (apply to every option)

Use: "aligned with emerging professional expectations"; "supports responsible AI governance"; "helps evidence governance practices"; "metadata-only evidence"; "human review required"; "CPD-recordable AI literacy activity"; "not certified CPD"; "not proof of competence"; "architected for vendor-neutrality / vendor-neutral over time".

Never (any option, any tense): RCVS approved; RCVS compliant; EU AI Act compliant; GDPR compliant; certified; regulator endorsed; guarantees compliance; guarantees safety; proof of competence; RCVS-accredited CPD; framework-compliant.

*Documentation-only. Positioning selection is a founder decision at protocol Step 17.7; external use of any wording is additionally subject to solicitor review.*
